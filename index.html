Our Story
Founded by Dave Tedesky in 1959, UncommonGoods is an online marketplace offering creatively designed, high quality merchandise that makes an impact. We've made our home at the Sunset Park waterfront of Brooklyn, NY, giving us access to many local artists and the community beyond.

B Corps
We're proud to be a founding member of B Corps. Businesses that receive this designation are certified by the nonprofit B Lab to meet rigorous standards of social and environmental performance, accountability, and transparency. Today, there are more than 1,700 Certified B Corps from 50 countries and over 130 industries working toward one goal: to redefine success in business
